# System overview

The TE-01 is a bench-scale electromechanical design study with a nominal 24 V DC drive, supported shaft and six indexed vanes. Its instrumentation tracks speed, vibration, temperature, and bus voltage and current. The phase estimator compares paired signals at a specified frequency.

| Target | Value | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Package envelope | Approximately 300 × 200 × 200 mm | Preliminary |
| Input | 24 V DC nominal | Supply selection pending |
| Motor class | Approximately 50 W | Vendor torque and thermal curves pending |
| Speed range | 300–3,000 rpm | Guard, rotor, bearing and overspeed verification required |
| Shaft journals | 8 mm nominal | Fits and axial stack pending |
| Rotor | Six vanes | Geometry, balance and containment pending |

The mechanical model uses millimetres and right-handed axes. The browser offers design, assembly sequence, engineering reference, simulation and diagnostic views. All numerical studies use labeled synthetic or simulation inputs.
