# Requirements baseline

Revision 0.1 follows [TE01_MASTER_SPEC.md](TE01_MASTER_SPEC.md) and the supplied [BOM](bom.csv). The BOM defines 30 canonical records. Section 2 defines nine canonical assemblies. Section 4 groups drive components and combines UPD/CSC for service ordering. The registry keeps the nine Section 2 owners, with Drive represented as a functional grouping under CSC. TE-029 has one registry owner, ABA, with connections and placements at the other joint sites. The source BOM grouping remains available in each record.

The coordinate origin is the projection of the midpoint of the shaft span between the journals onto the top baseplate plane. X follows the shaft toward the positive-X journal, Y is transverse, and Z is vertical. The coordinate system is right-handed. The shaft axis runs above Z = 0. The transverse model uses Y, with Z available for a later second degree of freedom.

Target dimensions and operating values come from the master specification. Scene coordinates, colors, proportions and separation vectors are schematic. Masses, tolerances and released CAD are unassigned.
