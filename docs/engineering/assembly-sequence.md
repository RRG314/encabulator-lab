# Assembly and service sequence

The digital sequence represents mechanical relationships for design review. Physical service requires energy isolation, verified standstill and a released procedure.

| Order | Exploded group | Inspection or alignment checkpoint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Guard access and interlock | Window integrity, latch geometry and service clearance |
| 2 | Cooling fan, duct and filter | Flow direction, bracket engagement and cable restraint |
| 3 | Electronics and harness | Connector keying, conductor clearance and labels |
| 4 | Channel insert and timing pickup | Rotor envelope clearance and pickup spacing |
| 5 | Six vane inserts and hub | Matched assembly set, defined retention and balance history |
| 6 | Shaft and rotor retention | Journal condition and axial locating arrangement |
| 7 | Bearing assemblies and pedestals | Bearing seats, co-axis and shaft rotation at isolated inspection |
| 8 | Flexible coupling and drive | Axial group length, clearances and qualified misalignment limits |
| 9 | Isolation and base | Datum condition, isolation compression and joint integrity |

Assembly review proceeds in reverse order. The provisional exploded paths support part identification. Tool access, load support, fastener sequence and tightening values are release decisions.

## Fastener schedule

TE-029 covers the base/feet, pedestal, motor mount, guard and service-bay attachment sites in the model. The registry quantity is a set. The CAD instance count describes representative joints. Thread pitch, grade, grip, coating, lubrication, preload and torque are pending joint-specific qualification. Catalog or measured fastening characteristics are required before establishing tightening procedures.
