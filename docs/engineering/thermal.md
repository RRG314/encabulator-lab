# Thermal architecture

The Panametric fan is driven independently of the rotor. The removable duct and filter guide flow toward the casing. The controller enclosure provides ventilation and strain-relieved cable routing. The bus supply, driver and motor each require a thermal boundary and a qualified probe location.

Probe locations in the register are the motor case and one bearing housing. The integrated simulation shows temperatures for motor and controller nodes. Instrument observations and simulated state use separate labels. Measure sensor offset and lag, enclosure and ambient temperature, flow, pressure loss, and electrical load over the qualified envelope.

## Prototype thermal test

1. Establish calibrated temperature and electrical power channels at defined locations.
2. Record stabilized ambient and idle conditions, with airflow state documented.
3. Measure a guarded power step under approved operating limits. Record both transient and equilibrium observations.
4. Identify node time constants and conductances, reconcile input and output power, and record fit quality.
5. Evaluate blocked-filter and stalled-fan scenarios within an approved safety procedure.
