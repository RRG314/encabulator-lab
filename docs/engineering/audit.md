# Engineering audit

Baseline surveyed at commits ef8255c and 261f199. The original inputs are preserved as the requirements specification and BOM. The repository contained 30 validated part records, nine canonical assemblies, 52 links, a millimetre-based parametric procedural mesh library and placement tests. The 3D browser, simulations, editable solids, manufacturing review sheets and Pages workflow were open.

The expanded directives introduce TE-100 through TE-900 as functional group identifiers. They are cross-references over the baseline ownership hierarchy. Keep original part IDs stable. Represent brackets, rails, grilles, sensor mounts, board hardware and retention features as subgeometry of their parent catalog part until the design assigns independent procurement or maintenance records.

The revision 0.1 DGS baseline puts an unqualified compliant element under the stationary drive. A separate travel-limited torsional concept is a design study with its own parameters. The baseline torque path uses TE-030. Any torsional prototype requires materials, fatigue, containment and travel validation before installation.

Rotor diameter, bearing fits, shaft shoulders, retainer method, guard load capacity, installed mass, fastener grades, supply fault current and pinouts are unresolved. The proposed envelopes are design inputs. Revision 0.1 drawings support dimensional review. Manufacturing release requires selected hardware, tolerances, load cases, verified assembly clearances, electrical fault review and controlled commissioning.
