# Component interchangeability and design comparison

| Domain | Turbo Encabulator TE-01 | Retro Encabulator RE-01 | Decision basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beam and journal | 8 mm nominal journal, 96 mm ideal support spacing | 12 mm nominal journal, 164 mm support spacing | Bearings, pedestals and shaft are model-specific. |
| Vanes and hub | Six radial inserts on a nominal 38 mm hub body | Six pitched 52 mm chord inserts on a separate keeper-equipped hub | Common topology with separate geometries and attachments. |
| Power and controls | 24 V class, bench instrumentation | 48 V class, raised cabinet and independent supervisory I/O | Final interface compatibility is open. |
| Cooling | Guarded independent fan and duct on shared foundation | Independent axial fan, slotted shroud, filter and taper | Selection and fan curves are model-specific. |
| Phase | Synchronous two-channel analysis | Separate module and shared timebase with relative quality gate | Algorithm primitives are shared. |
| Reciprocating load | Ventilated rotor assembly | Guarded crank-slider and water-loop scenario | Dedicated RE-01 subsystem. |

Candidate service tools may be common if the selected fastener drive and approved procedure match. A verified cross-model commercial substitution has yet to be established.
