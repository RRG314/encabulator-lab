# Retro Encabulator RE-01 implementation log

1. Audited the Turbo Encabulator registry, shared viewer, technology stack, CAD source and Pages workflow.
2. Established a separate RE-01 part namespace, 15 owning groups, source traceability and fixed mounting datums.
3. Generated and checked independent FreeCAD/OpenCascade bodies, STEP/STL/GLB, drawing set and BOM.
4. Integrated the two assembly routes, part-level tool records, diagnostics, operating console and comparative studies.
5. Established automated software and publication verification for the research release.

The hardware decision register remains open for installed fastener selection, material certificates, bearings, electrical safety architecture, measured fan and motor curves, linkage reduction choice, detailed swept clearances and water-loop pressure qualification.
