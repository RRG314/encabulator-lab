# Historical terminology and relationship traceability

Source R1 is the [Rockwell presentation transcript, page 1](https://ugc.futurelearn.com/uploads/files/b2/fb/b2fb36c2-a048-4244-a105-7817c2cde3bf/Rockwell_TRANSCRIPT.pdf). Paragraphs below are counted from the opening paragraph. RE-01 diagrams and values carry their own engineering basis.

| R1 position | Term or relationship | RE-01 interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| ¶2 | Unilateral phase detractors and synchronized cardinal gram-meters | Synchronous phase estimator with signal-quality gating and separate acquisition timebase. |
| ¶3 | Dodge gears and bearings, Reliance Electric motors, Allen-Bradley controls and Rockwell monitoring | Bearing and drive interfaces, generic control cabinet and software monitor. The reference brands supply historical context. |
| ¶4 | Named interaction of electrical reluctance and capacitance | Conservation of energy, motor conversion and accounted losses govern the RE-01 model. |
| ¶5 | Prefabulated amulite, logarithmic casing, inline bearings and Panametric fan | Machined foundation, removable guard, coaxial journals and independent axial cooling stage. |
| ¶5 | Six hydrocoptic marzelvanes fitted to the ambifacient lunar waneshaft | Six pitched rotor inserts mechanically indexed about a common supported axis. |
| ¶6 | Lotus-o-deltoid winding in panendermic semi-boloid slots | Twelve proposed slot groups and separate copper and steel packages. |
| ¶6 | Non-reversible tremie pipe and differential girdle spring with a reported conductor connection | One-way low-head fluid circuit and a separate torsional link. Each has its own physical domain. |
| ¶7 | Drawn reciprocating dingle arm used for a specified motion mode | Crank, connecting arm and guided slider driving a demonstration fluid load. |
| ¶8 | Operation of Milford trunnions | No RE-01 trunnion application or equipment specification has been established. |

The proposed dimensions, fluids, operating ranges, material grades, simulations and controls originate in the project design. The transcript supplies no quantitative validation.
