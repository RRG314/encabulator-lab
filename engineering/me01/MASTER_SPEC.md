# Metachronic Encabulator ME-01

**Polychronous Differential Synchronization Configuration**

**Revision 0.1 · design geometry · calculated and simulated evidence**

ME-01 couples a primary brushless-driven waneshaft, a concentric output sleeve, a compliant torsional connection, six indexed variable-pitch marzelvanes, and orthogonal radial stabilization. The fluid circuit and independent air fan exchange heat at the drive and bearing boundaries. A regenerative controller allocates signed electrical power between torque conversion, loss, DC-link storage and a dissipative sink.

The output station accepts bounded test-load definitions for a dynamometer, centrifugal pump, compressor, generator, reflected drivetrain inertia and turbine test shaft. These are mechanical boundary models. Physical coupling, dimensions, structural load ratings, interface compatibility and protections remain engineering release items.
